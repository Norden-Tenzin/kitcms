"@kitcms/plugin-enquiries": minor

New `@kitcms/plugin-enquiries`: contact and booking enquiry forms defined in code, a public submit endpoint (strict validation, same-origin, size and per-IP rate limits, honeypot, optional Cloudflare Turnstile), an admin-only `submissions` collection, email notifications through core's email adapter, and an `EnquiryForm` Svelte component that works with and without JavaScript.
